Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about system procurement, installation, warranties, and ROI.

Most commercial digesters and municipal bio-drum plants achieve complete ROI capital payback within 14 to 36 months. Payback is driven by eliminated landfill hauling fees, avoided carbon/tipping surcharges, and secondary value generated from high-grade organic bio-fertilizer output.

Our systems operate under a closed-loop negative pressure design. Exhaust air passes through moisture condensation traps, activated carbon filters, and a biological wood-chip/microbial scrubber bed that neutralizes volatile organic compounds (VOCs) and ammonia before clean air is released.

Commercial in-vessel units require a standard 3-phase electric connection (380V/480V depending on capacity), a standard water inlet for automated washdown cycles, and a level concrete pad. Our municipal bio-drum systems include complete site layout engineering and foundation blueprints.

Yes! Our municipal engineering division assists city planning committees, waste management authorities, and consultants with feasibility studies, environmental impact reports, EPA compliance documentation, and RFP specification drafting.

Our thermophilic microbial starter inoculant is added periodically (typically once per month or quarter) to maintain optimal digestion speed. Under our Service SLA or EaaS package, microbial refills, preventive maintenance, sensor calibration, and software updates are automatically managed by EcoCompost technicians.

Absolutely. We offer a lab-scale pilot testing service where prospective clients send samples of their organic waste (food waste, sludge, agricultural byproducts) to our R&D facility for digestion speed, moisture reduction, and bio-fertilizer quality analysis.
