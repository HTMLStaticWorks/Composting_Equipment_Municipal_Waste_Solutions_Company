Who We Are

Pioneering Organic Waste Transformation

Founded by environmental engineers and waste management experts, EcoCompost Solutions was created to bridge the gap between heavy industrial waste processing and localized organic recycling.

Our proprietary continuous bio-drum rotary composters and rapid in-vessel digesters leverage thermophilic aerobic decomposition to digest organic food waste, green waste, and municipal sludge in 24 hours without hazardous emissions or odor.
